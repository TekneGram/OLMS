## I
School breakfast programmes can support learning when they provide nutritious food in a welcoming and reliable setting. Students who arrive at school hungry may find it harder to concentrate, participate in class discussions, and manage their mood during the morning. A well-organised breakfast service can reduce these barriers by offering simple meals before lessons begin. It may also help families whose schedules or finances make morning meals difficult to arrange at home. However, schools need to protect students from embarrassment by making the programme easy to access and available without stigma. Staff should also consider dietary requirements, food safety, and the time needed for students to eat calmly. A strong breakfast programme is not a complete solution to poverty, but it can give students a healthier and more equal start to the school day.
